# Code of Conduct

We want ConsoleUI to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, regardless of experience,
background, identity or hardware.

## Our standards

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling or publishing others' private information.
- Keep discussions on topic and free of sexualized language or imagery.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow
this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who behave inappropriately.

Report problems privately to the maintainers through a
[GitHub private security advisory](https://github.com/Two-Play/ConsoleUI/security/advisories/new)
or the contact listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. All reports are handled confidentially.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
